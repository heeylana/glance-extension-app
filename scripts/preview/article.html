MARKET WIRETUE 15 SEP

Chip demand outruns supply for a third straight quarter

BY R. OKONJO · 6 MIN READ

Orders for accelerator silicon rose again last quarter, and the backlog is now quoted in quarters rather than weeks. Nvidia said it has sold every part it can make through the end of the year, and two of its largest customers have begun pre-paying for 2027 capacity.

The squeeze is spreading outward. Apple has reportedly locked in packaging capacity a year ahead of its usual schedule, while Tesla told suppliers it would take any inference parts available at spot prices.

Analysts are split on how long the imbalance holds. The bull case rests on training clusters still being built out; the bear case is that a single quarter of softer orders would leave the whole chain holding inventory it paid a premium for.

"Nobody in this industry has ever been punished for ordering too early. That is the whole problem."
